The seven day-one views

Static mockups for issue #12. Read the spec in README.md next to this file. Every page shares one stylesheet built on shadcn/ui's default tokens, so each part maps to a real component. Press ? on any page for its keys, and G then a letter to jump between views.

1. Today18 proposals, why each was picked, pick up to 5. Shows what is still being emailed from earlier days. 2. LinkedIn listPeople past the email budget, the role to name, and a log of requests, accepts, messages and replies. 3. OutboxA reply holding a company at the top, a blocked draft, a recruiter warning, a follow-up, and the limit at 20 of 30. 4. Needs youAll five review triggers with one-click fixes, including one waiting 11 days. 5. No-go listCompanies and people kept out, with reason, evidence and source. 6. Company pageBrowserbase, held by a reply: roles, people, emails, evidence and imported history. 7. Done companiesCrusoe, Retell AI, Distyl AI and a referral, with replies per month on top.

Web experience (#33): for your approval

Today becomes a plan of about 7 people with a finish line, and three new places show progress, wins and why a company ranks where it does. Each page has a Mockup state switch in its top bar for the states worth seeing. The old Today above stays as the list of all proposed companies.

Today as a planThe day rail of 7 people, Lena Ortiz ready with her reason, note and links. R and P log, the row stays put, the count moves. Find someone, email row, the 7-day question, the watch list, and a win arriving. The finish lineAll 7 done: what you did, tomorrow's preview and the week so far. A second state carries one person to tomorrow. ProgressToday against the plan, then the week or month as a ledger: counts beside their denominators and one thin bar. A first-week state shows counts with no rates. A quiet weekNo replies this week: your work first, your own usual reply rate second, the counts last and muted. WinsEvery referral, interview and interested reply, newest first, with what you sent. States for a new win and for no wins yet. Company page: ScorePylon's 0.74 taken apart: eight signals with points, share, the reason and its source, one neutral and one cost.
