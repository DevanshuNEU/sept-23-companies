# Email finders for a "this posting is for me" pipeline: what users say, what tests show, what the APIs allow (research, 2026-11-06)

Second half of `docs/research/2026-11-06-email-sourcing.md`. That file covered vendor pricing pages. This one covers real-user experience, independent tests, people search, MCP servers, data sources and terms. It does not repeat the price tables.

**Labels.** **USER** = a review or post by a customer. **INDEPENDENT TEST** = a test not run by a finder vendor (none passed cleanly, see section 2). **VENDOR TEST** = a test run by a vendor that sells a finder. **PRIMARY** = the vendor's own docs, terms, repo or pricing page. **UNVERIFIED** = could not read it, or only a secondary site says it. *inference* = my reasoning. Every page was read on 2026-11-06 unless a date is given.

**What I could and could not read.**
- Reddit: `reddit.com` and `old.reddit.com` both refused the fetch tool, and the search tool blocks `reddit.com`. I read **zero** Reddit threads. Every Reddit claim below is second-hand from a vendor page and is marked UNVERIFIED.
- G2 and Capterra returned 403. G2 figures come from search-result snippets only.
- Trustpilot loaded for Hunter, Prospeo, Anymail Finder, Findymail, Tomba and Apollo. Icypeas, LeadMagic, Enrow and Datagma have no Trustpilot page at the URLs I tried.
- Hacker News (Algolia API) loaded but holds almost nothing useful and nothing recent (section 3, Anymail Finder).
- Product Hunt: Icypeas has no reviews.
- Page text came through a summarizing fetch tool. I rechecked the arithmetic on every table I quote. Where numbers disagreed I say so.

## 1. Short answer

No independent test exists. Every head-to-head was run by a vendor, and they contradict each other. The least conflicted is a Clay test of 1,075 contacts at companies under 500 employees: Datagma, Hunter, Icypeas, Prospeo and LeadMagic all scored 96.8% to 97.7%, so they are tied. Review volume is thin. Prospeo (4.6 on Trustpilot, 132 reviews), Hunter (4.4, 357) and Anymail Finder (4.8, 149) have the most.

Best fit: **Prospeo**. Its people search filters by title, seniority and department, then reveals email, with an official MCP, for about $49. Fallback with a cleaner data origin: **Hunter**. Paying is worth it, but run a 20-company bake-off on free tiers first. Prospeo's records carry LinkedIn member IDs, so Devanshu must rule on his LinkedIn rule (section 4).

**What the tests agree on.** Findymail and Anymail Finder rank near the top in most tests (except Dropcontact's). Hunter ranks low on coverage in vendor tests that are not Hunter's, and high on quality in Clay's. Apollo covers the most and bounces the most (6.5% to 8.2% on its "verified" tag). Prospeo's coverage swings from 17% to 76% depending on input type and who ran it. Icypeas finds fewer but gets very few wrong.

**What they cannot answer.** None of these samples is seed to Series B startups of 10 to 100 people on Google Workspace. Clay's SMB set (under 500 employees) is closest. Catch-all handling is a coin flip in every test and is best-effort by their own admission.

### Icypeas

- **USER, count 1.** G2 listing: one review, 5.0, praises find rate and support (search snippet, 2026-11-06, [g2.com/products/icypeas/discuss](https://www.g2.com/products/icypeas/discuss)). Trustpilot has no page at `icypeas.com` or `www.icypeas.com` (404). Product Hunt: "Be the first to leave a review" ([producthunt.com](https://www.producthunt.com/products/icypeas/reviews)). One review is not a trend.
- **Second-hand, UNVERIFIED.** A search summary says one Reddit practitioner found it "middling" as a first finder but useful to cut bounces on "unknown" results, and another used it as a fallback. I could not find the threads. Hackceleration's page says it found "no independent user reviews substantial enough to judge accuracy" ([hackceleration.com](https://hackceleration.com/labs/best/email-finder)); that site says it did not open accounts.
- **Tests.** Clay SMB 97.46% and the cheapest cost per valid email ($0.0132). Anymail test: only 49.0% coverage but 0.5% false positives, the lowest. Own test: 79% found, 1.33% bounce. *Inference:* finds fewer, but what it returns is safe. That suits a one-click approval flow.
- **People search (PRIMARY, [find-people](https://api-doc.icypeas.com/reference/find-people.md)).** Filters `currentJobTitle`, `currentCompanyWebsite` and `currentCompanyName`, each with include and exclude lists of up to 200, plus location. Seniority and department are "not listed". 1 to 200 results per page. Count is free; "each result fetched costs 0.02 credit". No email in the result. A second call, email search, costs 1 credit if found. Title matching is "contains" per its own blog, so "engineering manager" works, "recruiter" works, "senior" alone is too loose.
- **MCP (PRIMARY).** Official: `https://mcp.icypeas.com/mcp`, API key entered on a redirect page, 21 tools (count only, names not on the pages I read), Claude Code works once the connector exists in Claude.ai ([setup guide](https://www.icypeas.com/blog/connecting-icypeas-mcp-and-claude-ai-a-step-by-step-guide), posted 2026-04-10). Pricing page says every plan includes MCP. The first report said no MCP was listed; that was wrong. Maintenance: no repo, no release date. UNVERIFIED.
- **Data source (PRIMARY).** GDPR statement: contact data comes "from open sources", and "Employee profiles and company profiles come from an external data provider: Avanteer". LinkedIn is not named. Avanteer's domain is parked for sale, so I could not read its own source. Find People returns `profileUrl`, "the URL to the lead's LinkedIn profile", and LinkedIn URNs, so the people half is LinkedIn-shaped (*inference*). Icypeas also sells a Sales Navigator extractor.
- **Terms.** Terms page 404, not read. Pricing page: credits "roll over... never expire, even if you downgrade or cancel". No refund line.
- **Verdict: maybe.** Cheapest and safest finds, but title-only people search and likely LinkedIn-derived profiles.

### Prospeo

- **USER, count about 16 read, of 132.** Trustpilot 4.6 ([trustpilot.com/review/prospeo.io](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/prospeo.io), read 2026-11-06). Low-star themes: privacy, 2026-10-23 "This company sold my contact information to a set of English companies" and 2026-09-10 "I keep getting spammed"; accuracy, 2026-06-24 "Incorrect basic data on my company" (support said it cannot edit "computer generated" data), 2026-05-19 "60-70 % email finding capabilities". 2026-06-24 (4 stars): UK hit rate "decent but not outstanding". 2026-07-28: "A bit Expensive from Apollo, but the data is more accurate". No billing or cancellation complaint in anything I read. **Astroturf flag:** Trustpilot shows a cluster of short 5-star reviews from single-review accounts between 2026-06-21 and 06-30. The profile says Prospeo "hasn't replied to negative reviews". Prospeo also publishes hundreds of "tested" comparison pages (`prospeo.io/s/...`) that rank Prospeo first. Treat those as marketing, and I did not use them as evidence.
- **Tests.** Mixed. Clay SMB 97.03%, "over 76%" coverage. Anymail test 45.2% coverage, 3.7% false positives. Tomba's test 16.9%, mostly because company-name search nearly failed (0.9%). *Inference:* always search by domain, which Knock has.
- **People search (PRIMARY, [search-person](https://prospeo.io/api-docs/search-person), [filters](https://prospeo.io/api-docs/filters-documentation)).** Filters: `person_job_title`, `person_seniority` (C-Suite, Director, Entry, Founder/Owner, Head, Intern, Manager, Partner, Senior, Vice President), `person_department` ("Engineering & Technical", "Human Resources", ...), `company.websites` (up to 500 per request). "1 credit is spent per search request that returns at least one person", 25 per page, repeat pages free for 30 days. Email is "not returned"; Enrich Person with the `person_id` costs "1 credit per email found" and "You won't be charged if no results are found". No recruiting department value seen, so use title text for recruiters. These filters show no plan callout, but a `PLAN_REQUIRED` error is possible. This is the only tool where the three Knock groups map to filters one for one.
- **MCP (PRIMARY, [docs](https://prospeo.io/api-docs/mcp)).** Official. Hosted `https://mcp.prospeo.io` (OAuth 2.0 or API key), or local `npx -y @prospeo/prospeo-mcp-server` v1.1.0 (Node 18+). Claude Code command is in the docs: `claude mcp add prospeo --env PROSPEO_API_KEY=... -- npx -y @prospeo/prospeo-mcp-server`. Tools: `search_suggestions` (free), `search_person` (1 per page), `enrich_person` (1, or 10 with mobile), `bulk_enrich_person`, `enrich_company`, `bulk_enrich_company`, `search_company`, `get_account_info`. No verify tool (every email is pre-verified, `verification_method` "SMTP" or "BOUNCEBAN"). Repo `prospeo-v2/prospeo-mcp-server` pushed 2026-10-18, MIT, 0 stars, not archived.
- **Data source.** Not stated anywhere I could read. Docs say only an "extensive up-to-date database". The person object has `linkedin_url`, `linkedin_member_id`, `headline`, `skills` and `job_history`, which is LinkedIn-profile-shaped (*inference*). Terms, privacy policy and trust-center pages render client-side and returned only titles. UNVERIFIED. Two Trustpilot privacy complaints suggest the data is not collected from the person.
- **Terms.** UNVERIFIED (page did not render). A secondary review says credits "reset on renewal and do not roll over" ([supered.io](https://www.supered.io/blog/email-finder-tools/), a competitor).
- **Verdict: yes, top pick, conditional** on the LinkedIn ruling and a free-tier check.

### Hunter

- **USER, count 357 on Trustpilot, 4.4** (read 2026-11-06, [trustpilot.com/review/hunter.io](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/hunter.io)). Recent: 2026-11-05 "Responsive, patient, and technically sharp support"; 2026-10-26 credits it with lower bounces. The 19 one and two-star reviews on page one: billing, 2026-09-22 "hidden fee for email warmup" (Hunter says it is on the pricing page and refunded); credits, 2025-11-05 "hunter will charge you for EVERY SINGLE query, even the unknown ones"; cancellation, 2025-09-13 "there's no cancel button"; accuracy, 2026-04-11 "60%+ bounce rate", 2026-01-13 "Over 50% of the names & email addresses ... NO LONGER WORK AT THE FIRM", 2026-05-08 "only about 7% of the results were actually usable". Hunter disputes several, saying the Mailtracker charges involve a separate service. Mixed, volume is high, and about a fifth of the sample is negative.
- **Tests.** Anymail test: 57.6% coverage, 9.3% false positives, 86.1% accuracy. Tomba test: 37.6%. Lobstr: 281 of 1,000. Dropcontact (second-hand): 11.2% hard bounce. Clay SMB: 97.58% confidence (second best), coverage not stated. The tests that ran real sends or three verifiers rate it weakest on bounces.
- **People search (PRIMARY, [API v2](https://hunter.io/api-documentation/v2)).** Domain Search takes `seniority` (junior, senior, executive), `department` (19 values), `job_titles`, `type`. Returns 10 emails by default, up to 100 with `limit`. Cost: "In the Domain Search, 1 credit is used for each email found" ([pricing](https://hunter.io/pricing)), so 10 emails is 10 credits; Bulk Domain Search is 1 credit per up to 10. Repeats count once. Seniority has no "manager" or "staff" level, so groups 2 and 3 need title text. It returns only people with an email Hunter already holds, which for a 10 to 30 person startup is often few (*inference*; one 2026 review says accuracy is "weaker for very small companies", UNVERIFIED). Email Finder is 1 credit and free on a miss. Verifier is 0.5 credit.
- **MCP (PRIMARY).** Official remote server `https://mcp.hunter.io/mcp`, Streamable HTTP, API key in `Authorization: Bearer` or `X-API-KEY`. The docs page I read gives Claude Desktop and OpenAI examples and no tool list or Claude Code line (*inference:* works with `claude mcp add --transport http`). The older `hunter-io/hunter-mcp` repo is archived ("no longer maintained", last push 2025-07-07, 8 stars). Community servers exist (Meerkats-Ai, Vinkius): unofficial.
- **Data source (PRIMARY, [hunter.io/our-data](https://hunter.io/our-data)).** "We analyze millions of websites daily"; "650M public sources"; guesses come from "public data ... at the domain name level". LinkedIn is not mentioned. The cleanest answer of any tool for the LinkedIn rule.
- **Terms ([hunter.io/terms-of-service](https://hunter.io/terms-of-service)).** API use allowed; "Abuse or excessively frequent requests ... may result in the temporary or permanent suspension". "You must not create more than one account." "Hunter does not provide refunds". "Your monthly or annual subscription will automatically renew unless you cancel it." Credits "reset monthly and don't roll over" (pricing page).
- **Verdict: yes, fallback.** Cleanest data origin and one call returns emails, but thin on tiny startups and has the most billing complaints of the cheap three.

## 4. Best single tool, and a fallback

**Best single tool: Prospeo.** It is the only one whose people search maps to all three groups with enums (Founder/Owner, Head, Manager, Director, Senior, "Engineering & Technical", "Human Resources") and a domain filter. One search is 1 credit per 25 people, an email reveal is 1 credit and free on a miss, and its official MCP has a documented Claude Code command and a repo pushed 2026-10-18. About 6 credits per posting (3 searches plus 3 reveals, *my arithmetic*) puts 1,000 addresses near 1,200 credits, inside the $49 plan. Evidence for: Clay SMB 97.03% with "over 76%" coverage, 4.6 on Trustpilot over 132 reviews with no billing complaints. Evidence against: 45.2% coverage in Anymail's test, 16.9% in Tomba's, privacy complaints, an unreadable terms page, a profile format with LinkedIn member IDs, and a Trustpilot review cluster that looks prompted.

**Fallback: Hunter** (if the LinkedIn ruling goes against Prospeo, or Prospeo's free tier misses on his targets). Domain Search takes `department`, `seniority` and `job_titles` and returns emails in one call. Its data page says public web only. Cost: 1 credit per email found, $49 for 2,000, remote MCP at `https://mcp.hunter.io/mcp`. Expect fewer hits at 10 to 30 person startups and more billing friction.

**If neither works:** Apollo's free people search plus Icypeas or Anymail Finder for the email, as a two-tool waterfall. Apollo's bounce and billing risks apply.

**LinkedIn, for Devanshu to decide.** No vendor says its people data is from LinkedIn, and none says it is not. These return LinkedIn profile fields: Prospeo (`linkedin_url`, `linkedin_member_id`), Icypeas (`profileUrl`, LinkedIn URNs), Findymail (`linkedinUrl`), LeadMagic (`profile_url`). Hunter and Tomba point to public web pages. The first report's reading was that calling a vendor with a name and domain stays inside Knock's rule, because Knock does no scraping. Whether a vendor's LinkedIn-shaped records also pass is a new call.

**Before paying.** Run 20 companies from the last month of postings through Prospeo, Hunter, Icypeas and Apollo free tiers (about 50 to 100 free credits each, from the first report). Count groups found per company and addresses a free verifier flags. That settles the one thing no test here covers: 10 to 100 person startups.

**Terms to avoid.** One-account rules (Hunter, Apollo) apply when stacking free tiers. LeadMagic, Enrow and Apollo give no refunds. Anymail Finder and Findymail are the friendliest on credit and refund terms.
