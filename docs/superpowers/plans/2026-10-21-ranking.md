# Ranking and hide rules Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Turn the store's postings, evidence and decisions into one stored score and one reason list per company, with lanes, a hide policy that says no only with evidence, a versioned config, and a "what moved" diff between two ranker versions.

**Architecture:** A loader builds `CompanyFacts` per company from the store in a few bulk queries (best posting first, then evidence). One module per signal turns facts into a value in -1.0 to 1.0 and a one-line reason. `score_company` applies the weights from `outreach/ranking/config.toml`. A separate hide policy calls `outreach.rules.passes()` for the posting level and adds the company level (no-go, size over 300 with evidence, a final sponsorship refusal). The `rank` job computes everything with no write lock held, then writes `company_score`, any no-go rows and the size review items in one transaction.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.14 (Homebrew), standard library only in `outreach/`, SQLite STRICT tables, `tomllib`, `unittest`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-20-opportunity-pipeline-design.md` (sections 3, 4, 5.4, 8, 9, 11, 12, 16, 17, 18). Issue #6 and its 2026-10-21 scope-change comment; #9 "Changes, 2026-10-21". Depends on the foundation plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-21-pipeline-foundation.md` (issue #30): `outreach/ranking/types.py` (`CompanyFacts`, `SignalValue`, `Signal`), tables `company_score`, `work_tier`, `company_post`, `watched_page`, `decision.provisional`, evidence facts `lca`, `cap_exempt`, `staffing_risk`, `funding`, `headcount`, review kind `size_check` with `raised_by 'ranker'`, `job_run.job 'rank'`, and Task 6 (`tests/contracts.py::check_signal`, `tests/pipeline/harness.py::PipelineHarness`). ## Review Focus

1. **Unknown size and unknown stage score exactly neutral**, never "small", never hidden, never a guess promoted to evidence. A guess over 300 stays shown, is ranked last and raises `size_guess`. (Tasks 3, 7, 10.)
2. **A posting that cannot be trusted never hides a company:** a lifted no-go is never re-added or re-applied; a refusal in an intern, off-lane or non-US posting, a provisional refusal, or a posting on a board with an open "board match doubtful" item never writes `no_go`. (Tasks 3 and 4.)
3. **Junk pay text:** "Competitive", "$150", "$5,000 sign-on", "£80,000", hourly ranges, "$120K to $150K", a remote role or an unlisted city. It never invents a level; the result is neutral unless the employer's LCA history says otherwise. (Task 2.)
4. **A company with nothing:** no postings, no evidence, only hidden postings, only postings on a doubtful board, a merged-away duplicate. It is scored without a crash, keeps empty lanes, and stays on the list. (Tasks 4, 5, 8, 11.)
5. **A re-run changes nothing and a failed run changes nothing:** the same version over the same store writes identical rows, and an exception mid-run leaves the previous version's rows and the no-go table untouched. (Task 11.)

## Decisions this plan makes (the spec and issue leave them open)

- **`CompanyFacts` is the foundation's and is not changed.** Signals also need the best posting's title, level tier and sponsorship stance, the pay note, weak size flags, the work tier and the reach route. They live on `RankFacts(CompanyFacts)` in `outreach/ranking/facts.py`, with defaults, so a signal that only needs `CompanyFacts` fields works on either. Open question 1.
- **Eleven signals, two more than design section 9's nine:** `posting_sponsors` (a posting that says it sponsors), which the foundation's flow invariant ("a live new-grad posting that says it sponsors ranks first and its reasons name both") needs and the interim spike scored; and `large_later_stage`, split out of `small` so the bottom push is one visible weight. Open question 2.
- **Score** is the sum of `weight * value` over the signals. Two signals carry a weight of 200, more than every positive weight added together (120): `staffing_risk` and `large_later_stage`. A staffing-flagged employer that is not promoted and a large later-stage company therefore sort below every other company and stay on the list (design 5.1 and 9). A config test pins this.
- **Lanes** (design section 8, in this order, a company holds every one that applies): `level_role` (a visible engineering posting with `level_ok = 1`); `intern_junior` (a visible intern posting that is not student-only, so `eligibility = 'ok'`); `reason_no_role` (no `level_role` and no `intern_junior`, and money raised within 18 months, or junior H-1B filings in the last two fiscal years, or a `watched_page` row); `any_us_role` (no `level_role` and no `intern_junior`, a visible engineering posting with `level_ok = 0`, and the company is not large later-stage). A company with no lane is scored and listed, never hidden. An HN post that asks for email counts toward reach only, not toward a lane.
- **"Visible" posting** means open, decided under the current ruleset, on a trusted board, and `passes()` is true. Intern postings go through `passes(dataclasses.replace(d, lane='engineering'))`, because `passes()` drops interns and the design (section 8) changes that for lane 2 without restating the hide policy.
- **Best posting** per company: lane (`level_role` > `intern_junior` > `any_us_role`), then freshest (`posted_on`, else the date of `first_seen_at`), then a sponsorship stance of `yes`, then `opt_only`, then the rest, then the highest `posting_id`.
- **Trusted board.** An open `review_item` with `kind = 'flagged'`, a `board_id`, `raised_by = 'crawler'` and a reason starting `board match doubtful` makes that board's postings count for nothing: not for lanes, the best posting, pay, the weak many-postings flag, or any hide. The company keeps every other signal and stays visible. When he resolves the item, the board counts again. (#21 sets this item; coordinator update 2026-10-21.)
- **Hide order** for a company: an active `no_go` row; size over 300 with evidence (`confirmed` or `estimate`, `num_low > 300`, from `size`, else `headcount`); a sponsorship refusal. A refusal is a visible-lane posting with `lane = 'engineering'`, `us <> 'no'`, `sponsorship = 'no'` on a trusted board (the 2026-10-21 rule). It is final when its decision has `provisional = 0`: the rank job then writes `no_go` (`reason 'no_sponsorship'`, `source 'rules'`, `quote`, `source_ref` = posting id) unless any `no_go` row, lifted or not, exists for the company. It is provisional when every refusal is provisional: the company is excluded from the ranked list with the label "hidden, pending reader", and nothing is written. A lifted `no_go` row means a refusal hides nothing. The over-300 hide is computed on every run and is not written to `no_go`, so a corrected size lifts it.
- **Merged companies:** every read follows `company.merged_into` (a chain is followed to its end). A merged-away company has no score row; its postings, evidence and people count for the survivor. Companies with status `closed` or `removed` are not scored.
- **Size.** `size_low` and `size_high` come from `company_fact` `size`, else `headcount`. Weak "not small" flags (more than 80 trusted open postings, 25 or more software LCAs in one fiscal year, the company's domain in `shared_key` as a `domain`, stage in a later round with no size) make the `small` value -0.3 and raise `size_check` only for companies in the top 100 by score (design 5.4: look it up where it matters). No flag ever hides. Known large parents are not in the store, so that flag is not built.
- **Pay level.** A posted range in `posting.pay` is read with `pay.py` against `outreach/ranking/wages.toml` (SOC 15-1252, OFLC 2026-27, the six areas in the research); the level is taken at the range midpoint (config `basis`, "minimum" is the alternative) and the note shows both. A location that matches no area, or no pay, falls to the employer's LCA history (at least 3 junior filings: level I if at least half were level I, else level II). Neither known: `pay_level = None`, neutral. Pay that only the body states is not visible here (open question 3).
- **Reachable person:** a `person` row at the company with `role_kind` in engineer, manager, founder or alum and no active `no_go` row (value 1.0); or, failing that, a `company_post` with `asks_email = 1` posted in the last 90 days (value 0.6). Recruiters do not count (#9 changes).
- **Work tier:** `work_tier.tier = 3` with `promoted = 0` counts as a staffing-flagged employer for the `staffing_risk` signal even if the evidence row is missing; promoted, a `high` risk is graded -0.5 instead of -1.0.
- **Stored JSON.** `company_score.lanes` is a JSON list. `company_score.signals` is `{"values": {name: {"value", "weight", "points", "reason"}}, "reasons": [reason, ...], "hide": null or {"kind", "reason", "provisional"}, "flags": [...], "best_posting_id": int or null, "ignored_postings": int}`. `reasons` holds the non-zero signals, largest `abs(points)` first. A hidden company keeps its score; list queries filter on `hide`.
- **Ranker version** hashes `config.toml`, `wages.toml` and the code that changes a score (`config.py`, `types.py`, `common.py`, `pay.py`, `roles.py`, `facts.py`, `hide.py`, `score.py`, `signals/*.py`). `__init__.py`, `version.py`, `scores.py`, `review.py`, `job.py`, `diff.py` and `__main__.py` are outside the hash, and a test fails on any file in neither group.
- **Versions kept:** the three newest by `scored_at`. The reader's queue reads "the newest `scored_at`", so every row of a run shares one `scored_at` and is written in one transaction.

## Task 3: Shared loaders and the hide policy

**Files:**
- Create: `outreach/ranking/common.py`, `outreach/ranking/hide.py`
- Test: `tests/test_ranking_hide.py`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `RankerConfig` (Task 1); `Decision`, `passes` from `outreach.rules`; `stored_decision` from `outreach.rules.rebuild`; `append_event` from `outreach.store`.
- Produces in `common.py`:
  - `BOARD_DOUBTFUL_PREFIX = "board match doubtful"`.
  - `canonical_ids(conn) -> dict[int, int]`: every `company_id` to the end of its `merged_into` chain (a cycle stops at the first repeat, never loops).
  - `doubtful_board_ids(conn) -> frozenset[int]`: `board_id`s with an open `review_item` of `kind = 'flagged'`, `raised_by = 'crawler'` and `reason LIKE 'board match doubtful%'`.
