# Ranking and hide rules Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Turn the store's postings, evidence and decisions into one stored score and one reason list per company, with lanes, a hide policy that says no only with evidence, a versioned config, and a "what moved" diff between two ranker versions.

**Architecture:** A loader builds `CompanyFacts` per company from the store in a few bulk queries (best posting first, then evidence). One module per signal turns facts into a value in -1.0 to 1.0 and a one-line reason. `score_company` applies the weights from `outreach/ranking/config.toml`. A separate hide policy calls `outreach.rules.passes()` for the posting level and adds the company level (no-go, size over 300 with evidence, a final sponsorship refusal). The `rank` job computes everything with no write lock held, then writes `company_score`, any no-go rows and the size review items in one transaction.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.14 (Homebrew), standard library only in `outreach/`, SQLite STRICT tables, `tomllib`, `unittest`.

## Controller ruling (2026-10-22, Devanshu). It overrides the task text and the 2026-10-21 rulings below where they disagree.

An interim list showed postings from 2016 and felt low-confidence. Devanshu ruled two things.

1. **Stale postings never count as live.** A stale posting is one posted more than 12 months ago, or not seen live
   on its board in the latest successful read of that board; a failed read never makes one stale. Ranking does not
   derive this. It calls `outreach.crawl.stale.load_stale` (#21 Task 16a, which owns the rule and the
   `stale_after_months` config value) once per run and passes the result down. A stale posting is not a role: it
   gives no lane, is never the best posting, feeds no `live_role`, `posting_sponsors` or `pay_level`, is not counted
   as an open posting, and neither refuses nor sponsors in the hide policy. It is listed last on the company
   (`Roles.stale`, `signals.stale_postings`) with its reason, and the company gets a flag. Stale is never a hide:
   the company stays visible and ranked.
2. **Confidence is explicit and higher.** Every signal returns an `Assessment`: its value, its reason and a
   `Confidence` (`high`, `medium` or `low`, plus the basis in one plain line). Every stored component carries both,
   and so does the final rank reason, which says how much of the score rests on facts and which part rests on
   guesses or missing data. Ranking prefers higher-confidence evidence: a positive component counts at 1.0, 0.85 or
   0.5 of its weighted value by confidence (config `[confidence]`); a negative component always counts in full, so a
   doubtful warning never gets softer. Unknown is `low`, never `high`. The grading rules are in Decisions and Task 2a.
3. **Where the work goes.** New Task 2a (confidence grades). Stale and confidence edits are inside Tasks 1, 3, 4, 5,
   6, 7, 8, 11, 13, 14 and 15. Task numbers are unchanged.

## Review Focus

1. **Unknown size and unknown stage score exactly neutral**, never "small", never hidden, never a guess promoted to evidence. A guess over 300 stays shown, is ranked last and raises `size_guess`. (Tasks 3, 7, 10.)
2. **A posting that cannot be trusted never hides a company:** a lifted no-go is never re-added or re-applied; a refusal in an intern, off-lane or non-US posting, a provisional refusal, or a posting on a board with an open "board match doubtful" item, or on a `stapply_name` board not yet read once, never writes `no_go`; a refusal at a company with another posting that says it sponsors never hides it. (Tasks 3 and 4.)
3. **Junk pay text:** "Competitive", "$150", "$5,000 sign-on", "£80,000", hourly ranges, "$120K to $150K", a remote role or an unlisted city. It never invents a level; the result is neutral unless the employer's LCA history says otherwise. (Task 2.)
4. **A company with nothing:** no postings, no evidence, only hidden postings, only postings on a doubtful board, a merged-away duplicate. It is scored without a crash, keeps empty lanes, and stays on the list. (Tasks 4, 5, 8, 11.)
5. **A re-run changes nothing and a failed run changes nothing:** the same version over the same store and the same `today` writes identical rows, and an exception mid-run leaves the previous version's rows and the no-go table untouched. (Task 11.)
6. **A stale posting is never a live role and never a hide, and a failed read never makes one stale.** A 2016 posting still listed by its board gives no lane, no `live_role`, no sponsor credit, no pay level, no refusal and no `no_go`; the company stays listed with a flag and the posting is shown last with its reason; a board whose last read errored keeps its postings live (graded `medium`). (Tasks 3, 4, 5, 6, 11, 13.)
7. **Confidence is stated everywhere and never inflated:** every `Assessment`, every stored component and the rank reason carry a level and a basis; unknown is `low`; a `guess` evidence row never reads `high`; the discount touches positive points only, so an uncertain warning counts in full; a hand-locked row is the only way a guess reads `high`. (Tasks 2a, 5, 6, 7, 8, 13.)

## Decisions this plan makes (the spec and issue leave them open)

- **`CompanyFacts` is the foundation's and is not changed.** Signals also need the best posting's title, level tier and sponsorship stance, the pay note, weak size flags, the work tier and the reach route. They live on `RankFacts(CompanyFacts)` in `outreach/ranking/facts.py`, with defaults, so a signal that only needs `CompanyFacts` fields works on either. Open question 1.
- **Eleven signals, two more than design section 9's nine:** `posting_sponsors` (a posting that says it sponsors), which the foundation's flow invariant ("a live new-grad posting that says it sponsors ranks first and its reasons name both") needs and the interim spike scored; and `large_later_stage`, split out of `small` so the bottom push is one visible weight. Open question 2.
- **Score** is the sum of `points` over the signals, where `raw_points = weight * value` and `points = raw_points * factor(confidence)` when `raw_points` is positive, else `raw_points` (ruling 2026-10-22; `factor` is 1.0, 0.85 and 0.5 for high, medium and low). Two signals carry a weight of 200, more than every positive weight added together (120): `staffing_risk` and `large_later_stage`. A staffing-flagged employer that is not promoted and a large later-stage company therefore sort below every other company and stay on the list (design 5.1 and 9). A config test pins this.
- **Lanes** (design section 8, in this order, a company holds every one that applies): `level_role` (a visible engineering posting with `level_ok = 1`); `intern_junior` (a visible intern posting that is not student-only, so `eligibility = 'ok'`); `reason_no_role` (no `level_role` and no `intern_junior`, and money raised within 18 months, or junior H-1B filings in the last two fiscal years, or a `watched_page` row); `any_us_role` (no `level_role` and no `intern_junior`, a visible engineering posting with `level_ok = 0`, and the company is not large later-stage). A company with no lane is scored and listed, never hidden. An HN post that asks for email counts toward reach only, not toward a lane.
- **"Visible" posting** means open, decided under the current ruleset, on a trusted board, not stale (ruling 2026-10-22), and `passes()` is true. Intern postings go through `passes(dataclasses.replace(d, lane='engineering'))`, because `passes()` drops interns and the design (section 8) changes that for lane 2 without restating the hide policy.
- **Best posting** per company: lane (`level_role` > `intern_junior` > `any_us_role`), then freshest (`posted_on`, else the date of `first_seen_at`), then a sponsorship stance of `yes`, then `opt_only`, then the rest, then the highest `posting_id`.
- **Trusted board.** An open `review_item` with `kind = 'flagged'`, a `board_id`, `raised_by = 'crawler'` and a reason starting `board match doubtful` makes that board's postings count for nothing, and so does a board with `found_by = 'stapply_name'` and `last_read IS NULL` (its first read has not happened, so the check has not run): not for lanes, the best posting, pay, the weak many-postings flag, or any hide. The company keeps every other signal and stays visible. When he resolves the item, the board counts again. (#21 sets this item; coordinator update 2026-10-21.)
- **Hide order** for a company: an active `no_go` row; size over 300 with evidence (`confirmed` or `estimate`, `num_low > 300`, from `size`, else `headcount`); a sponsorship refusal. A refusal is a visible-lane posting with `lane = 'engineering'`, `us <> 'no'`, `sponsorship = 'no'` on a trusted board (the 2026-10-21 rule). A stale posting is never a refusal and never a sponsor (ruling 2026-10-22). It is set aside when any other open posting of the company on a trusted board reads `sponsorship` `yes` or `opt_only`: no `no_go`, no hide, the flag "postings disagree on sponsorship" and a `flagged` item (ruling 9, Task 10). It is final when its decision has `provisional = 0`: the rank job then writes `no_go` (`reason 'no_sponsorship'`, `source 'rules'`, `quote`, `source_ref` = posting id) unless any `no_go` row, lifted or not, exists for the company. It is provisional when every refusal is provisional: the company is excluded from the ranked list with the label "hidden, pending reader", and nothing is written. A lifted `no_go` row means a refusal hides nothing. The over-300 hide is computed on every run and is not written to `no_go`, so a corrected size lifts it.
- **Merged companies:** every read follows `company.merged_into` (a chain is followed to its end). A merged-away company has no score row; its postings, evidence and people count for the survivor. Companies with status `closed` or `removed` are not scored.
- **Size.** `size_low` and `size_high` come from `company_fact` `size`, else `headcount`. Weak "not small" flags (more than 80 trusted open postings, 25 or more software LCAs in one fiscal year, the company's domain in `shared_key` as a `domain` (`SHARED_DOMAIN_FLAG`: it lowers `small` but raises no `size_check`, ruling 11), stage in a later round with no size) make the `small` value -0.3 and raise `size_check` only for companies in the top 100 by score (design 5.4: look it up where it matters). No flag ever hides. Known large parents are not in the store, so that flag is not built.
- **Pay level.** A posted range in `posting.pay` is read with `pay.py` against `outreach/ranking/wages.toml` (SOC 15-1252, OFLC 2026-27, the six areas in the research); the level is taken at the range midpoint (config `basis`, "minimum" is the alternative) and the note shows both. A location that matches no area, or no pay, falls to the employer's LCA history (at least 3 junior filings: level I if at least half were level I, else level II). Neither known: `pay_level = None`, neutral. Pay that only the body states is not visible here (open question 3).
- **Reachable person:** a `person` row at the company with `role_kind` in engineer, manager, founder or alum and no active `no_go` row (value 1.0); or, failing that, a `company_post` with `asks_email = 1` posted in the last 90 days (value 0.6). Recruiters do not count (#9 changes).
- **Work tier:** `work_tier.tier = 3` with `promoted = 0` counts as a staffing-flagged employer for the `staffing_risk` signal even if the evidence row is missing; promoted, a `high` risk is graded -0.5 instead of -1.0.
- **Stored JSON.** `company_score.lanes` is a JSON list. `company_score.signals` is `{"values": {name: {"value", "weight", "raw_points", "points", "reason", "confidence", "basis"}}, "reasons": [reason, ...], "hide": null or {"kind", "reason", "provisional"}, "flags": [...], "best_posting_id": int or null, "ignored_postings": int, "stale_postings": [{"posting_id", "title", "reason"}], "confidence": {"level", "basis"}, "rank_reason": {"text", "level", "basis", "line"}}`. `reasons` holds the non-zero signals, largest `abs(points)` first; `confidence` is the rank confidence and `rank_reason.line` is the one line a list shows. A hidden company keeps its score; list queries filter on `hide`.
- **Ranker version** hashes `config.toml`, `wages.toml` and the code that changes a score (`config.py`, `types.py`, `common.py`, `pay.py`, `confidence.py`, `roles.py`, `facts.py`, `hide.py`, `score.py`, `signals/*.py`), then the bytes of the crawler's `outreach/crawl/stale.py` and the text `stale_after_months=<n>` from the crawl config (a different stale rule or cutoff is a different score). `__init__.py`, `version.py`, `scores.py`, `review.py`, `job.py`, `diff.py` and `__main__.py` are outside the hash, and a test fails on any file in neither group.
- **Versions kept:** the three newest by `scored_at`. The reader's queue reads "the newest `scored_at`", so every row of a run shares one `scored_at` and is written in one transaction.
- **Stale postings** (ruling 2026-10-22). `compute_rank` calls `load_stale(conn, today, max_age_months=stale_after_months)` once (the argument is `None`, so the crawler's config value, unless a test passes one) and passes the mapping to `load_roles` and `load_hide_inputs` and nowhere else; no ranking module re-derives staleness. A stale posting (open, and either posted over 12 months ago or absent from the latest successful read of its board) is removed from: lanes, the best posting, `live_role`, `posting_sponsors`, posted pay, `open_count` (so the many-postings flag), and the refusal and sponsor sets of the hide policy. Stale never hides a company and never lowers a score by itself: it only removes the credit a live role would have given. Postings that would have been visible are kept in `Roles.stale` (lane, then age, so a list shows them last), copied to `RankFacts.stale_postings`, stored as `signals.stale_postings` with each reason, and summarized in a flag such as "1 stale posting not counted as live: posted 2016-03-02, over 12 months" (with more than one: "<n> stale postings not counted as live: <newest reason>"). A failed board read never makes a posting stale (the crawler's rule; ranking asserts it again on a store state left by an errored read). A stale posting on a doubtful board counts as ignored, not stale.
- **Confidence** (ruling 2026-10-22). `Confidence` is a `level` (`high`, `medium`, `low`) and a `basis`, one plain line that says what the level rests on. Grades are computed once, in `facts.py`, by the pure functions of `confidence.py` (Task 2a); a signal copies `facts.grade(self.name)` into its `Assessment` and never grades by itself. Rules:
  - Evidence signals (`everify`, `cap_exempt`, `lca_junior`, `small`, `large_later_stage`, `funding`, `staffing_risk`): the winning `company_fact` row's `confidence` column (`confirmed` high, `estimate` medium, `guess` low); a hand-locked row is high; evidence observed more than `evidence_old_days` (365) ago drops one level and the basis says how old; no row is `low` with the basis "no <fact> evidence". `lca_junior` takes the weakest of the rows it sums. `large_later_stage` takes the stage row when the stage triggered it, else the size row. `staffing_risk` from work tier 3 with no evidence row is `medium` (the basis names the tier reason).
  - `live_role`: `high` when the best live posting was listed at its board's last good read (`last_read` `ok` or `unchanged`) and has a posted date; `medium` when the board's last read failed (basis: last listed on <date>) or the posting has no posted date (its age is a guess from first seen); `low` when the board was never read by the platform (an import) or the posting has no board. With no live role: `high` when a board of the company read `ok`, `medium` when only failed reads exist, `low` when no board is known (roles are unknown, not absent). With only stale postings: `high` when the newest is stale by age (a posted date is a fact), `medium` when it is stale only by absence (a first empty read can be a glitch).
  - `posting_sponsors`: `high` when the best posting's decision is final (`provisional = 0`, a verified reading); `medium` when it rests on the phrase rules alone; `low` when there is no live posting.
  - `pay_level`: `high` for a posted range whose level is the same at the minimum and the midpoint; `medium` when the level changes inside the posted range, or for LCA history with at least `lca_pay_medium_filings` (10) junior filings; `low` for LCA history with 3 to 9 filings, or no pay at all.
  - `reachable_person`: `high` for a person with a `team` source; `medium` for a person with no source or an HN post that asks for email; `low` when none is recorded.
- **Rank confidence and the rank reason** (ruling 2026-10-22). Each non-zero component has `raw_points` (before the discount) and a confidence. The rank confidence looks at the share of `sum(abs(raw_points))` that rests on each level: `low` when nothing is non-zero, or the largest component is `low`, or the `low` share is at least `rank_low_share` (0.5); `high` when the largest component is `high` and the `high` share is at least `rank_high_share` (0.7); else `medium`. Its basis reads `<h>% of the points rest on high-confidence facts`, then `, <m>% on medium-confidence facts` when `m` is not 0, then `, <l>% on guesses or missing data (<up to three low signals, largest first, underscores as spaces>)` when `l` is not 0 (`l` and `m` are rounded, `h` is the rest); with nothing non-zero it is `no signal has evidence`. The rank reason is the first `reason_count` (3) reasons joined by `; ` (or `No signal has evidence yet`) and `line` is `<text> (<level> confidence: <basis>)`. A list shows `line`, so it says why and how sure.
