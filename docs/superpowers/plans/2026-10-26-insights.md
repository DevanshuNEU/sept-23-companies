# Insights Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Show Devanshu which companies gave him interviews and what they have in common, list the top-ranked companies most like them, add a small one-way ranking lift toward companies like them, and export the outcome data for his own analysis.

**Architecture:** A new standard-library package `outreach/insights/` builds one outcome row per canonical company from `outreach_touch`, `application`, `company`, `company_fact`, `board` and `posting_decision` in a handful of set-based queries, and turns the interview rows into plain-word findings with raw counts. A twelfth ranking signal, `interview_like`, compares each listed company with the interview companies on four traits (size band, stage band, role level band, board system) and can only add points; the `rank` job loads the interview profile once per run from outcomes only. `GET /api/insights` reads the dataset, the findings and the stored ranking, and a new Results screen in `web/` shows them. A CLI exports the dataset as CSV or JSON.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.14 (Homebrew), standard library only in `outreach/insights/` and `outreach/ranking/`, SQLite STRICT store, `tomllib`, `csv`, `unittest`; FastAPI and pydantic in `outreach/api/` only; React 19, TypeScript, Vitest and Playwright in `web/`.

**Spec:** Issue #81 (its "To build" and "Done when" lines are the requirements). Issue #9 (the pinned decision record wins over every doc here). `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-20-opportunity-pipeline-design.md` sections 9 (ranking: weights in config, a reason per signal, a weight change is a config commit with a new ranker version), 11 (outcomes) and 16 (honest numbers, minimum samples, "the system visibly learns", calm). The opportunity-first rule: never hide a reachable company, so the lift may only raise a score, never lower one, hide one or write a no-go. Built code this plan extends: `outreach/ranking/` (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-21-ranking.md`, its rulings win where the code differs), `outreach/outcomes/report.py` and `outreach/api/queries/progress.py` (the Findings rules of `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-21-web-experience.md` Tasks 13 to 15: minimum samples 30 requests and 20 per group, counts without percent below them), `outreach/identity.py` (merges and `raise_merge_candidate`), `docs/design/README.md` (the visual system and keyboard map).

## Decisions this plan makes (the issue leaves them open)

- **An interview company** is a canonical company with any of: a live touch (`undone_at IS NULL`) with `interview_at` or `offer_at` set; an `application` with `outcome` `interview` or `offer`; or `company.close_reason = 'interview'` (the importer closes Done companies that way). Referrals and replies are kept in the dataset as their own outcomes but do not make an interview company (Pending Devanshu Q9).
- **The dataset universe** is every canonical company with at least one live touch, an application or `close_reason = 'interview'`, whatever its status. Companies he never reached are not rows: the comparison group for "what they share" is "reached, no interview".
- **Best outcome** per company, best first: `offer`, `interview`, `referred`, `replied`, `accepted`, `none` (`OUTCOME_ORDER` reversed). The touch that carries it is the earliest touch at that level; its channel, person role kind and lane (from `context`) are the row's `best_channel`, `best_role_kind` and `best_lane`. An outcome from `application` only has channel `application`; one from the company status only has no channel (shown "No recorded way in"). The importer's placeholder touch for an interview with no way in has channel `other`; it reads the same way.
- **Time to reply** is days, one decimal, from the earliest replied touch's `sent_at` to its `replied_at`; `None` without a reply.
- **Facts are as known now, not at the time.** The winning `company_fact` row across the merged family (`outreach.ranking.common.fact_order_key`, `size_order_key`), because most evidence was recorded after the touches by the import and the sources; "at the time" would leave nearly every fact unknown. Each fact keeps its confidence in the export (Pending Devanshu Q5).
- **Bands** (`outreach/insights/bands.toml`, one source for the dataset, the findings and the tilt):
  - size from `size_high`, else `size_low` (the `small` signal's rule): `1 to 50`, `51 to 300`, `301 to 1,000`, `over 1,000` (tops `[50, 300, 1000]`);
  - stage: `seed or earlier` (`pre_seed`, `seed`, `yc`, `early`), `Series A or B` (`series_a`, `series_b`), `later private` (`series_c`, `series_d`, `series_e_plus`, `late_private`), `public`;
  - role level: `intern` (a decision with lane `intern`), `entry level` (tiers `new_grad`, `early_career`, `swe1`), `SWE II` (`swe2`), `level not stated` (`open`, `not_stated`), `senior`;
  - board system: the `board.ats` of the company's board with the most open postings (active boards first, then the lowest `board_id`); no board is unknown;
  - HQ state: the two capital letters after the last comma of the `hq` fact ("San Francisco, CA" is `CA`), else unknown.
  Unknown is `None` and never equals anything, not even another `None`.
- **Role level of a touch** comes, in order, from the touch's `posting_id`, the company's application `posting_id`, then the newest engineering or intern posting of the family that was open when the touch was sent (`first_seen_at <= sent_at` and `closed_at` null or later); else unknown. `level_basis` says which (`touch posting`, `application posting`, `open at the time`, empty).
- **What they share** (Task 5). For each of nine dimensions (`size_band`, `stage_band`, `level_band`, `board_system`, `best_channel`, `best_role_kind`, `sponsorship`, `everify`, `hq_state`), over the interview companies: `of` is how many there are, `known` how many have a value. A dimension is `unknown` when `known < share_min_count` (3); `shared` when its most common value has at least `share_min_count` companies and at least half of `known`; otherwise `mixed`. The comparison with "reached, no interview" companies is drawn only when both groups have at least `group_min` (20) companies with a known value (`enough`); otherwise the line says too few to tell. No rate is computed below the minimum anywhere.
- **Possible duplicates** are listed, never merged here. Pairs among dataset companies (both survivors) on: the same non-guess `domain` fact (basis `insights_domain`, `strong`); the same `company`-class registrable domain in their people's email addresses (`insights_email_domain`, `likely`; `outreach.sources.keys.registrable_domain` and `classify_domain`); the same `company_name_key` of 4 or more characters (`insights_name_key`, `likely`); one name of 2 to 5 letters equal to the initials of the other's words, "gm" and "General Motors" (`insights_initials`, `weak`). `python -m outreach.insights duplicates --raise` turns each pair into one `merge_candidate` item through `outreach.identity.raise_merge_candidate` (explicit `confidence=`), which he settles in Needs you; nothing else writes.
- **The tilt signal** is `interview_like`, weight key `[weights] interview_like`, default `6.0` (the smallest existing weight, `funding`). Formula, with the four traits `T = (size_band, stage_band, level_band, board_system)`:
  - for a listed company `c` and each interview company `i` other than `c` itself (same canonical id is skipped), `m(c, i)` is the number of traits in `T` where both values are known and equal;
  - `best = max over i of m(c, i)`; the value is `best / 4` when `best >= min_matches` (2), else `0.0`. So the value is 0, 0.5, 0.75 or 1.0, never negative;
  - with fewer than `min_interview_companies` (3) interview companies in the profile, the value is 0 for everyone ("Fewer than 3 interview companies so far");
  - the reason names up to two interview companies that reach `best` (newest first interview, then name) and the traits shared with the first one: `Like Retell AI and Distyl AI, where you interviewed: 51 to 300 people, Series A or B, Ashby board` (`and 3 more` when more tie);
  - confidence is never `high`: `low` while the profile has fewer than `confident_at` (20) interview companies (basis `13 interview companies so far, under 20 is a small sample`), else `medium` (basis `24 interview companies`). With the ranker's discount (`[confidence] factor_low 0.5`, `factor_medium 0.85`) a lift today is at most 3 points, at most 5.1 at 20 or more.
  This signal is the one exception to "a signal copies `facts.grade(name)`": its grade belongs to the profile, not to the company's facts, so `InterviewProfile.confidence` carries it.
- **Feedback-loop guard** (all six hold together):
  1. outcomes only: the profile is built from interview and offer outcomes (above), never from scores, proposals, picks, plans or touches without an interview;
  2. bounded: the value depends on the nearest interview company, not on how many there are, so more interviews at one kind of company never raise a lift above `weight`;
  3. capped: the config loader refuses `interview_like` above `max_weight_share` (0.1) of the top score, the sum of every other positive weight (120 today, so at most 12.0);
  4. one way: the value is in 0 to 1, the ranker's discount touches positive points only, and `decide_hide` never sees the signal, so no hide and no `no_go` can change;
  5. minimum: under 3 interview companies it lifts nobody;
  6. off switch: weight `0.0` (allowed for this weight only) keeps the value and reason stored for lookalikes and adds no points.
  A company's score never drops. Its rank can drop by the number of companies lifted past it, which `rank --diff-with` shows before the weight is committed (Task 10).
- **Ranker version.** `signals/interview_like.py` and `config.toml` are hashed already. The version also hashes the insights files that change a score: `INSIGHTS_FILES = ("bands.toml", "bands.py", "dataset.py", "similar.py")`; the rest of `outreach/insights/` is in `NOT_INSIGHTS` and a test fails on a file in neither. New interview outcomes change scores under the same version, as `today` does for stale postings; the stored reason names the companies, and the job summary records how many interview companies it used.
- **Stored features.** `company_score.signals` gains a top-level `features` object `{size_band, stage_band, level_band, board_system}` (additive; old rows simply lack it). Lookalikes read it, so the API never re-derives a company's traits.
- **Lookalikes** (Task 11): among the top `lookalike_pool` (300) rows of `ranked_list` (no hide), the companies with a similarity value above 0 against the current profile, ordered by `best` matches (more first), then rank; at most `lookalike_count` (15). Interview companies are left out; companies already reached stay, tagged "Reached".
- **Claude's read.** No new MCP tool. `python -m outreach.insights summary` prints the interview companies, what they share and the possible duplicates in the live checkout, and the existing `ranked_companies` MCP read already shows each company's reasons, the tilt's included (Pending Devanshu Q6).
- **Config homes.** Tilt numbers live in `outreach/ranking/config.toml` `[interview_like]` (hashed). Band definitions live in `outreach/insights/bands.toml` (hashed). The screen's numbers live in `outreach/insights/config.toml` (not hashed): `group_min = 20`, `share_min_count = 3`, `lookalike_pool = 300`, `lookalike_count = 15`, `export_dir = "~/Desktop/outreach-scratch/insights"`. A test pins `group_min` to `ApiConfig.finding_group_min`.
